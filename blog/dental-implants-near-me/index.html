Why Patients Search for Dental Implants

Patients consider dental implants for several reasons:

  • Replacing one or more missing teeth for improved chewing and appearance
  • Increasing stability compared to dentures
  • Preserving jawbone and preventing tooth shifting
  • Desiring a natural look and feel

Implants are just one of several replacement choices. Elm Ridge Implant and Family Dentistry also offers full arch options and guidance to help you make an informed decision.

What Affects the Answer for Dental Implants?

Getting dental implants is a decision based on several factors specific to each patient, including:

  • General oral health and gum condition
  • Jawbone density and structure
  • Number and location of missing teeth
  • Existing dental work or restorations

That’s why a thorough evaluation is important before any recommendations can be made. Our team at Elm Ridge will discuss your best options after a personalized exam.

FAQ

Should I ask a dentist about dental implants?

Yes, it’s a good idea to ask your dentist about dental implants if you have missing teeth or are considering tooth replacement. Your dentist can assess your dental and health history to see if implants could be a suitable solution.

Can you give an exact answer online?

Unfortunately, online information can only provide general guidance. The best treatment option depends on a personal dental exam and diagnostic images, which can’t be done virtually. Schedule an appointment at Elm Ridge Implant and Family Dentistry for recommendations specific to you.

Will insurance cover treatment?

Some insurance plans may partially cover parts of the dental implant process, but coverage varies. Our team can review your policy and explain your options during your visit.
